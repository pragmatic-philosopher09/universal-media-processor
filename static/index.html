    Why is the original “only” 1080p?

    Instagram and TikTok re-encode every upload and never deliver 4K: their CDNs top out at 1080p, usually 30 fps (YouTube does serve 4K/8K natively when the creator uploaded it; DeviantArt serves the original image file). Every downloader that reports 4K from Instagram is upscaling. This app is honest about it — it fetches the best rendition that exists, and the Enhance mode computes the 4K 60 fps version here. The default engine is classical signal processing (ffmpeg motion-compensated interpolation, Lanczos scaling, contrast-adaptive sharpening) — smooth and natural, but it cannot invent detail. The optional AI engine (RIFE + Real-ESRGAN neural networks) can, but it needs extra binaries and is experimental and unverified.
