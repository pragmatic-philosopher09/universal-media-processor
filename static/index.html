          Why is the original “only” 1080p, and what does Enhance do?

          Instagram and TikTok re-encode every upload and never deliver 4K: their CDNs top out at 1080p, usually 30 fps (YouTube does serve 4K/8K natively when the creator uploaded it; DeviantArt serves the original image file). Every downloader that reports 4K from Instagram is upscaling. This app is honest about it — it fetches the best rendition that exists, and the Enhance mode computes the 4K 60 fps version here. The default engine is classical signal processing (motion-compensated interpolation, Lanczos scaling, contrast-adaptive sharpening) — smooth and natural, but it cannot invent detail. The optional AI engine (neural networks) can, but it needs extra binaries and is experimental and unverified.

          What do the labels on results mean?

          Native / Your file: exactly what the source delivered. Upscaled, Interpolated and the AI labels mean pixels or frames were generated. Letterboxed, Cropped, Frame rate converted and Constant frame rate come from Standardize; converted frame rates repeat or drop frames rather than inventing motion. The manifest lists all of it per file, with checksums.
